> ye saalon ki zindagi jo maine guzari hai,
> har roz ankhon se mulaqat hote rehte hai.
> har ek nazar ke peechay ek aur shakal nazar ate hai,
> par in tamam nazron ke darmiyan,
> jab tumhari ankhon se mulaqat hui,
> jitna bhi dekha, unki gehrai kabhi khatam nahi hui.
> 
> shayad ye ek moajiza tha,
> kyunki bas tumhari tasveeron se hi toh mutasir tha dil.
> khwabon mein bhi tum aate ho,
> meri tanhai ko ek pal ke liye roshan kar dete ho.
> lekin us roshni ke baad bhi,
> dil ke kone mein ek khaali si bechaini thi.
> na jaane kab wo bechaini khatam honge,
> kab wo din aayega,
> jab sapne haqeeqat banenge,
> jab tumhare haath mere haathon mein honge.
> 
> tumhare haathon ki lakeeron mein,
> tumhari gehri, bepanah ankhon ke har raaz likhe honge.
> shayad tab main tumhe tumhari tarah mohabbat se dekh paunga,
> aur tumhe apni zindagi ka mehboob bana paunga.
> shayad tab tumhe bhi ehsaas ho,
> ke tumhare liye mera dil kitna betaab rehta tha.
